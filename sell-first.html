Selling and building at the same time

Sell first or build first? Start with your move-in date.

When you have a home to sell and a new one coming, the question isn't which comes first. It's when each step has to start so both land in the same month. Pick the month you want to be moved in, and we'll count backwards from there.

Jump to the countdown

How the timeline works

These are the numbers I'm seeing on Utah County listings right now. Every home is different, but this is a solid place to start planning.

1–2WEEKS

Declutter and small repairs

Before photos go up, most sellers want a week or two to pack up the extras, touch up paint, and knock out the small fixes a buyer's inspector would flag.

45DAYS

On the market

Most homes are sitting around 45 days from the day they list until they go under contract. Some go faster, some slower, but planning for 45 keeps you from getting squeezed.

30DAYS

Under contract to closing

Once you accept an offer, plan on about 30 days of escrow for the buyer's inspection, appraisal and loan to wrap up before you close and hand over the keys.

75 daysfrom listing day to sold, plus your 1 to 2 weeks of prep before that.

Builder rule

List as soon as you sign

Some builders want your current home on the market right after you sign their contract. That protects them if your sale falls through.

With this rule, the day you sign with the builder is roughly your listing day, so the timing of your build matters a lot.

Builder rule

List within 90 days of CofO

Other builders want your home listed within 90 days of the CofO (Certificate of Occupancy), which is the city's sign-off that the new home is finished and ready to live in.

Since 75 days fits inside that 90-day window, this rule usually lines up well with a sell-and-move-the-same-month plan.

Why count backwards

List too early and you can end up with nowhere to live while the new home finishes. List too late and you can end up carrying two mortgages. Starting from the month you want to move and working back tells you when each step needs to start.

Ask your builder which listing rule they use before you sign. If you're not sure yet, the countdown shows you both.
